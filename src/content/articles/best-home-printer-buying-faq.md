Choosing a home printer comes down to one question that most buying guides skip: how much do you actually print, and does photo quality matter to you? Below are direct answers organized around that framework, covering EcoTank, laser, and standard inkjet options.

## Matching printer type to how you actually print

- **Light, occasional printing (a few pages a month):** a standard cartridge inkjet, since tank ink risks clogging if it sits unused too long.
- **Heavy text/document printing, no photos:** a laser printer -- lowest cost-per-page, and toner doesn't dry out.
- **Heavy mixed printing including photos and color:** a refillable tank printer -- highest upfront cost, lowest ongoing cost.
- **Home office needing scan/copy too:** a multifunction printer in whichever ink system matches your volume above.
