## What to Look For in a Fountain Pen Under $50

When shopping for a fountain pen under $50, consider factors like nib size, ink compatibility, and build quality. A fine or medium nib is versatile for most writing tasks, while broader nibs are great for bold strokes. Look for pens that use standard ink cartridges or converters, offering flexibility in ink choices. Durability is also key—opt for materials like metal or high-quality resin that can withstand daily use.

Comfort is another important factor. A well-balanced pen with a comfortable grip can make long writing sessions effortless. If you’re interested in more detailed buying tips, our [fountain pen buying guide](/pillars/choosing-a-fountain-pen) covers everything you need to know.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are fountain pens under $50 reliable for everyday use?**
A: Absolutely! Many pens in this price range, like the LAMY Safari and Pilot Metropolitan, are built to last and perform well daily.

**Q: Can I use bottled ink with these pens?**
A: Yes, most fountain pens under $50 are compatible with converters, allowing you to use bottled ink for a wider color selection.

**Q: How do I maintain my fountain pen?**
A: Regular cleaning with water and occasional nib adjustments will keep your pen in top condition. Avoid letting ink dry out in the pen.
